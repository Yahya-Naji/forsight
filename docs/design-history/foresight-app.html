What is changing

The regional intrusion-set landscape is concentrated rather than diffuse. MITRE ATT&CK lists 176 active intrusion sets, of which 36 reference Middle East, Gulf or UAE targeting in their threat descriptions EV-ATTACK-001. One of these, Stealth Falcon, names the United Arab Emirates explicitly in its targeting description.

Connected defence architectures inherit the vulnerability surface of their control and navigation components. NVD returns 20 distinct CVE records across command-and-control, UAV, GNSS and SCADA keywords in the last 120 days EV-NVD-001, the most severe scoring 9.8.

What it means for the UAE

The UAE lacks adequate detection coverage against regionally-active intrusion sets targeting defence infrastructure, creating a material readiness gap.

Customer validation required
